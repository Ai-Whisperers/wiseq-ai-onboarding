# Lead-Gen Data Summary (no personal data)

*Our B2B market research for WiseQ. This file documents scale, sources, and method only. Raw contact datasets are kept private and transferred under GDPR terms — they are never published.*

## 🎯 Netherlands (complete, ready to use)

| Segment | Orgs scanned | With email |
|---|---|---|
| Special-ed schools (national) | 740 | 398 |
| School boards / bevoegde gezagen | 644 | 387 |
| Primary schools (Noord-Holland sample) | 150 | 87 |
| Key orgs (childcare chains, sector bodies) | 13 | 9 |
| **Total** | **1,547** | **857 orgs / 1,710 emails** |

Sources: DUO Open Onderwijsdata (official government register, CC-style open data); org websites (public contact pages). Method: register download → website crawl (homepage/contact/team) → email extraction with deobfuscation → junk filtering → role-address prioritization. Every row source-traceable.

**Whale networks** (one domain reaches many schools): 15 found, top: Aloysiusstichting (40 schools), De Onderwijsspecialisten (23), Ozeo (19), RENN4 (14), Auris (12 — hearing/language education), Leo Kanner (6 — autism specialists).

## 🏛️ France (banked for later-phase expansion)

64,715 education establishments with emails (in-register, no crawling) from data.education.gouv.fr — including 10,418 ULIS special-needs units flagged. Académie-domain structure: top 8 domains reach 27k+ schools.

## 🌐 EU register map (verified)

Flanders (one browser session to pull), Germany (16 Länder portals), UK (GIAS), Italy (MIUR), Poland (RSPO), Spain, Nordics, Estonia — total addressable ~250k schools. Method generalizes; FR/EE/IT/UK publish emails in-register.

## 📅 Compliance

- B2B role addresses (info@, directie@, bestuur@) under legitimate interest — standard NL practice; named-person outreach needs lawful basis
- Every send: sender identity + KvK + unsubscribe
- Datasets stored access-controlled; not committed to this or any public repo
