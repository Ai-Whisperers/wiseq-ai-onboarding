Built, not described

Three complete, scroll-driven storefronts — production-quality HTML, CSS and JavaScript against WiseQ's real 229-product catalog. No framework, no build step: open the file and it runs. Concept 03 makes the 77-bundle combinatorial problem into the brand's visual language — the constellation is the bundle graph.

01 · CinematicFull-bleed scroll narrative, 26,700 px. The catalogue as a film reel. Best for brand and storytelling.
02 · EditorialTypography-led product journal, 18,800 px. Feels like a specialist catalogue, not a shop.
03 · ConstellationThreehei bundle graph as a navigable starfield. The commercial one — makes ordering bundles feel inevitable.
These are demonstrations of craft, not a redesign proposal. Nothing here is deployed and nothing here overrides the live site. If WiseQ likes one, it becomes a scoped conversation with its own price — never a bundled extra.
