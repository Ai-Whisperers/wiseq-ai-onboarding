## ✉️ The longer version (when he asks "what exactly is this?")

Rob — quick background on what you're looking at.

**What it is.** A complete AI-operations proposal for WiseQ: stack blueprint, automation backlog, honest cost model, acceptance gates, and a decoded bundle spine (114 components + 25 bundles) from your public catalog. Nothing replaces Shopify, ChannelDock, or e-Boekhouden — every phase is additive and measurable.

**What it is not.** A storefront redesign or a rip-and-replace. The focus is orchestration: school orders, purchase intelligence, reviews, and gated writes.

**Why it matters for the pilot.** Phase 0 is read-only (stack map, EAN registry, shadow demo). Phase 1 is the school-order pipeline. Detail: [the full proposal](https://github.com/Ai-Whisperers/wiseq-ai-onboarding#readme).

**The ask, again.** Three things, none needing production access:

1. The **~30-page company manual** — so we map every tool to its connector
2. The **software inventory** — bookkeeping package, ChannelDock plan, 5X, mailbox provider
3. **A few anonymized school orders** — so the shadow demo mirrors reality

Send those and the Phase-0 deliverables (stack map, EAN registry v1, shadow demo) start the same day. Detail: [Next Steps →](../20-offer/03-next-steps.md)
