# Who you would actually be working with

*The question behind this page is fair: "Who exactly is doing this? You did this for a restaurant and a dental practice — why you?" Here is the honest answer, with names, roles, and what each person's day actually looks like. This page exists because a proposal without people is a document; a proposal with people is a relationship.*

---

## 👤 The people

### Ivan Weiss Van Der Pol — Founder & Principal Engineer

**Role on this engagement:** architect of the operations layer, owner of the blueprint (doc 04), and your primary technical contact.

- Runs AI Whisperers — a builder of AI-operations systems for specialist SMEs. Built the research dossier, decoded the bundle spine, and wrote the scroll engine behind the [site concepts](01-site-concepts.md) himself.
- **Previously shipped:** a production commerce backend for a bakery business (AI product imagery, automated price tracking and forecasting, a messaging-based ordering loop, QR-code payments parsed into XML invoices and inventory), and a dental-practice client engine (1,090 leads processed, a 400-file clinical encyclopedia). Those are the "restaurant and dental practice" references — different domains, same patterns: **extract → structure → decide → act, with human gates on money**.
- **How he works:** measurable phases, reversible steps, costs visible. The [guardrails](../20-offer/01-blueprint.md#-guardrails) section is his operating philosophy, not marketing copy.
- Works in English and Spanish natively; works in Dutch with tooling support — see the language note below.

### The agent organization — "the 116 agents"

**Role:** the 24/7 workforce. Not a metaphor: AIW runs an orchestrated agent fleet of ~116 specialized agents and 79 scheduled jobs across its own infrastructure — the same runtime this proposal would run on.

- **What that means for you:** the nightly purchase-intelligence job, the review-invitation engine, and the school-order pipeline would run on infrastructure that is *already running*, not assembled for the first time for your project.
- **What you saw in the session:** the live research and multi-agent orchestration demonstrated at the Grootebroek working session was this fleet working in real time.

### Domain specialists (on call, not on payroll)

Where the engagement needs named human expertise — NL contract law for the [terms](../60-governance/01-terms.md), lab certification for [compliance](../30-market/04-compliance.md), bookkeeping-vendor specifics — we bring in specialists under NDA rather than pretend generalists cover it. Doc 12's 31 specialist passes exist precisely because we would rather *simulate* 31 specialist reviews before building than improvise them after.

## 🗣️ The Dutch-language question, answered directly

An NL founder-led SME is right to ask. Here is the honest position:

| Layer | Status | What it means day-to-day |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Written Dutch — reading** | ✅ Full | Every public source in this repo — wiseq.nl, KvK, channeldock.com, Thuiswinkel terms, bol policies — was read in the original Dutch. Nothing relied on translation. |
| **Written Dutch — producing** | ✅ With review | We draft NL copy with AI assistance and **native review before anything customer-facing ships**. The [site concepts](01-site-concepts.md) were written in Dutch from the start. |
| **Spoken Dutch — meetings** | ⚠ Limited | Working sessions run in English (as ours did). We can bring a native-speaker colleague to milestone meetings if you prefer Dutch. |
| **All customer-facing output** | ✅ Native-gated | Review invitations, school offers, product copy — nothing goes out in Dutch without native review. That is a guardrail, not an aspiration. |

**We would rather tell you this now than have you discover it in week three.**

## 🔧 How the team scales up and down

- **Phase 0 (two weeks):** Ivan + the agent fleet. Fixed fee, no surprise headcount.
- **Phase 1:** same, plus specialist calls as the connector work needs them.
- **If volume grows:** the fleet scales — more agents, same architecture. Adding capacity does not mean renegotiating who does what.
- **If we are unavailable:** the [continuity plan](../60-governance/01-terms.md) covers handover — repo ownership, documented architecture, escrowed credentials, and a named successor operator. The boring page that makes this safe.
