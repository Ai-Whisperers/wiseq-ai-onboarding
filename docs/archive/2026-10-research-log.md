# What We've Done So Far

*Completed by AI Whisperers, 2026-10-21, all parent-side verified research. Raw artifacts live in our private workspace; this repo carries the findings.*

## 1. 🧭 Company & registry research

Verified legal entity, timeline, domain history, channels, reputation. Key findings:
- WiseQ is an **8-year marketplace veteran** (bol since June 2018), not the 1-year shop it appears
- The Sep 2025 Shopify rebuild was a replatform of an existing brand (adult audio → kids-tech pivot)
- Full report: [Company Profile](../10-client/02-company-profile.md) (raw: RESEARCH_COMPANY.md)

## 2. 🕰️ Catalog & data-quality audit

Programmatic audit of all 230 products (1,277 images, 228 SKUs):
- **0/230 EAN barcodes, 0/230 inventory tracking, 0/230 weights in Shopify** — stock truth lives in ChannelDock
- **77 combinatorial bundle-products (33%)** — adapter × cable × length × color; component→bundle mapping lives in human memory
- 20 products with empty descriptions; mixed SKU conventions; a real SKU bug (10.9" cases carrying 10.2" SKUs)
- Raw: RESEARCH_CATALOG.md + full products.json artifact

## 3. 💬 Marketplace & reputation research

- bol.com: 9.2/10, 312 articles (channel drift vs 230 shop products)
- Amazon.nl: legacy ASINs with review equity (Hero 4.4★×45) but stale English titles + missing EU compliance blocks
- Trustpilot 4.5/13 reviews (Oct 23), zero automated invitations; Scamadviser algorithmic 0-flag
- Raw: RESEARCH_MARKETPLACES.md

## 4. 📦 Marketing/tech-stack research

- 45-page SEO knowledge hub + 12 blog articles mapped; strong long-tail, absent from head-term listicles
- **agents.md + live UCP/MCP agentic-commerce endpoints documented** — WiseQ is ahead of nearly all NL retailers here
- No email ESP / review app detected; socials thin (FB 1.6K)
- Raw: RESEARCH_MARKETING_COMPETITION.md

## 5. 🧱 B2B market & lead generation (NL)

Built a full lead-gen pipeline on official DUO open data:
- **8,673 organizations** mapped (6,088 primary + 900 special-ed + 1,630 secondary + 55 mbo + 742 boards)
- **857 orgs with verified harvested emails** (1,710 rows): special-ed schools nationally, school boards nationally, NH primary sample, key orgs (childcare chains incl. 14 Humankind regional emails)
- **Whale-network discovery**: 15 email domains reaching 5–40 schools each (Aloysius 40, Onderwijsspecialisten 23, Ozeo 19, RENN4 14, **Auris 12 — hearing/language education, perfect product fit**, Leo Kanner 6 — autism)
- Methodology replicable; data private (GDPR), handed over on request

## 6. 🗺️ EU market scan

- **France: 64,715 education contacts pulled** from the national register (emails in-register, 10,418 ULIS special-needs units flagged) — banked for later-phase expansion
- Flanders register verified (CSV needs one browser session); DE/UK/IT/PL/ES/Nordics registers mapped
- ~250k-school addressable market; register-direct pattern (no crawling) for FR/EE/IT/UK
- Raw: EUROPE-PLAYBOOK.md

First working session with WiseQ leadership — documented in [Meeting Notes](../10-client/04-meeting-2026-10-21.md). Produced the [AI-stack blueprint](../20-offer/01-blueprint.md) and [automation backlog](../20-offer/02-automation-backlog.md) from it.
