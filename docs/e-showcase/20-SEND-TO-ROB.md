## ✉️ The longer version (when he asks "what exactly is this?")

Rob — quick background on what you're looking at.

**What it is.** Three complete, scroll-driven storefront concepts built against your **real 229-product catalog** (from wiseq.nl, images served locally). No framework, no build step — plain HTML/CSS/JS that loads fast and runs anywhere, including your phone. Each takes a different design direction:

- **01 · Cinematic** — dark stage, your products as hero objects. Film-grade hero imagery, scroll-driven camera moves.
- **02 · Editorial** — the "printed matter" direction: serif masthead, chapter structure, the full catalogue as a beautifully typeset table (all 229 products). Feels like a museum catalogue, not a webshop.
- **03 · Constellation** — the most ambitious: a WebGL scene where your products float as a navigable constellation, powered by the same three.js components used by large-scale product configurators. The bundle-recombination problem you told us about is literally the interface: bundles float as connected nodes.

**What it is not.** A redesign proposal. Nothing is deployed, nothing touches wiseq.nl, and none of this overrides anything. It exists for one reason: you asked what we can do — this is a faster answer than a deck.

**Why it matters for the pilot.** The demos prove the craft half. The other half is operations: the school-order pipeline (request → offer → invoice → ChannelDock) running as an agent pipeline on dummy data. Both halves are documented in the repo behind the demos — [the full proposal starts here](https://github.com/Ai-Whisperers/wiseq-ai-onboarding#readme).

**The ask, again.** Three things, none needing production access:

1. The **~30-page company manual** — so we map every tool to its connector
2. The **software inventory** — bookkeeping package, ChannelDock plan, 5X, mailbox provider
3. **A few anonymized school orders** — so the shadow demo mirrors reality

Send those and the Phase-0 deliverables (stack map, EAN registry v1, shadow demo) start the same day. Detail: [Next Steps →](../a-proposal/06-NEXT-STEPS.md)
