## 🗺️ Market position

- Specialty: kids headphones for home, travel, school — with 85 dB volume limiting, "hufterproof" durability, hearing protection
- B2B: schools (klassensets, laptop carts, invoice ordering), education retailers, childcare
- Two main direct competitors (per founder, 2026-10-21): small specialists with outdated websites ("90s" / "2000s"); plus big generalists (MediaMarkt, bol own-listings, Amazon)
- SEO: owns the long tail (autism/ADHD/schools guides), absent from head-term listicles
- EU expansion asset: ships NL+BE; Amazon EU seller account carries listings across .de/.fr/.it/.es

## 📌 In short

WiseQ is a **small, well-run Dutch specialist retailer** (founded 2018, 230 SKUs, Grootebroek) that already owns more modern infrastructure than most of its Dutch competitors — Shopify with a custom B2B layer, ChannelDock for fulfilment, agentic-commerce endpoints. The verified gaps are narrow and specific: **zero EAN codes on 230 SKUs**, and process work happening in Excel and the inbox. That combination is why we are proposing an *orchestration layer*, not a replatform.
