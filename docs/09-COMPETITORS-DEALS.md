# WiseQ Competitive Landscape & Deal-Model Research — 2026-10-22

*Third research wave: who WiseQ really competes with, how comparable AI-automation engagements are priced, and the tooling gaps confirmed by OSS search. Sources cited per section.*

## 1. 🧭 The real competitor map (not just headphone brands)

WiseQ's fight is on **three fronts**, and the research shows each has a different leader:

**Front A — kids-tech product brands (unit competition):**
| Brand | Position | Price point | Threat to WiseQ |
|---|---|---|---|
| **iClever** | **#1 kids-headphone brand in US online** (Euromonitor, Nov-2025); sold on Amazon EU | $21.99 wired → ~$50 ANC | The volume leader; undercuts WiseQ on Amazon.nl where their listing quality is weakest |
| **Puro Sound Labs** | Premium hearing-safety story (82% noise attenuation), strong US reviews | $50-65 | Owns the "parent-trust" positioning WiseQ also claims |
| **BuddyPhones** | Established kids brand, mid-price | ~$40-50 | Direct overlap on bol listings |
| **Alpine** (NL) | **"Grootste producent gehoorbescherming NL"** — Muffy Kids €24,95 B2B-priced at koptelefoon-onderwijs.nl for schools | €25 | The domestic A-brand for the school oorkap segment — WiseQ's 8 hearing-protection SKUs vs their school channel |
| **Xplora** (NL/EU) | GPS-watch category leader (XGO3/X6PLAY), heavy NL marketing | ~€169 | Owns the category where WiseQ's 9 GPS trackers compete |

**Front B — school suppliers (channel competition):**
- **Heutink ICT** — NL market leader in digitalizing primary/secondary education (~100 yrs, totaalleverancier incl. ICT). This is what a school's procurement officer compares WiseQ against: a full-catalog institution-grade supplier.
- **koptelefoon-onderwijs.nl** — a niche B2B shop selling Alpine Muffy school packs — proof the klassenset/oorkap niche has specialized incumbents.
- Implication: WiseQ can't out-catalog Heutink; it CAN out-niche them (safe-audio class sets with 24h delivery and B2B invoice terms via the staffelprijzen layer).

**Front C — the trust battlefield:**
Alpine has decades of brand trust; iClever has Amazon volume. WiseQ's levers: price (€19-28), agentic B2B UX, and the review engine + Thuiswinkel Waarborg plan (doc 08) to close the credibility gap.

## 2. 🕰️ How similar deals are structured (the pricing research)

What agencies charge for comparable work in 2026:

| Model | Range found | Fit for WiseQ engagement |
|---|---|---|
| Focused workflow build | **$3,000** | The EAN-spine + bol-drift audit as a paid pilot — exactly this size |
| Multi-agent custom system | **$50,000+** | The full stack (phases 0-3) |
| Agency retainer (ecommerce, all tiers median) | **$6,000-8,000/mo** (Shopify-specialist median; NL Plus-agencies €2,000-5,000/mo; basic from $500) | The ongoing-ops model after build |
| Outcome-based pricing | Pay per resolved ticket / per order processed (Sierra, Nevermined model write-ups) | **The differentiator for our pitch**: price per school-order processed or per listing reconciled — aligns incentives, low risk for Rob |
| bol certified partners | bol's own CPOP program lists integrators (webwinkelfacturen.nl for e-boekhouden coupling etc.) | Route to become a bol-certified integrator later — credibility + lead source |

**Recommended deal shape for WiseQ (from the research):** paid pilot ($3-5k: bundle spine + EAN fix + bol drift report + review engine live) → retainer (€2-4k/mo: orchestration ops + content + outreach) with **outcome components** (per school-order processed, per review captured). This matches the market's proven tiers while our cost basis (Hermes self-hosted, agent labor) runs 50-70% gross margin per the agency-economics research.

## 3. 💬 OSS wave 2 — the gaps that remain gaps

Six more GitHub topics searched (data in `data/oss/`): order-to-cash (28 repos, top 285★ — reference apps, no production-ready O2C), email automation (**BillionMail 15.9k★** — self-hosted, fixes the no-ESP finding), invoice parsing (0 repos ≥5★ → UBL/e-boekhouden parsing is custom agent work), product matching (0 repos → the bundle-spine matcher stays proprietary), Shopify CLI/theme (healthy), price intelligence (3 thin repos).

**Net insight:** every layer WiseQ needs either (a) doesn't exist as OSS → we build it thin and own it, or (b) exists as official API → we integrate it. Combined with wave 1 (doc 08): **the WiseQ stack has no installable competitor — which is precisely the moat we're selling.**

## 4. 📦 What WiseQ could do — the consolidated opportunity list

New opportunities this research adds to the backlog:

1. **Niche-bomb koptelefoon-onderwijs.nl pattern**: WiseQ already has school pages; the move is category-landing domination for "klassenset koptelefoons" + "oorkappen school" — the incumbent is a one-product shop, beatable on catalog + content.
2. **Bol certified-partner track**: after the Retailer-API build proves itself, apply to bol's CPOP program — certified integrator status compounds (leads + credibility).
3. **Outcome-priced ops**: school-order processing priced per order — the 4-hour mornings become a P&L line Rob can turn on/off.
4. **BillionMail self-hosted ESP**: €0/mo alternative to Klaviyo/Omnisend for the newsletter + review-ask flows (both currently absent).
5. **iClever counter-positioning on Amazon.nl**: their US strength = their EU complacency; refreshed listings (title/bullets/A+ content) + review velocity can win the .nl search shelf.
6. **Heutink counter-strategy**: don't out-catalog — out-deliver: 24h class-set delivery, invoice terms, agentic reordering (UCP endpoint lets school procurement agents reorder automatically — Heutink can't).
